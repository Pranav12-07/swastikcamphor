# Complete customer-site translation

## Scope
Translate the entire customer experience into English, Telugu, and Hindi. The staff/admin area remains in English.

## Changes
- Expand the central language dictionary to cover every customer-visible label, heading, instruction, validation message, empty state, status, and notification.
- Connect translation to all public pages: shop, product lists/details, blogs, about, contact, FAQ, policies, tracking, sign-in, chat, cart, and checkout.
- Connect translation to signed-in customer pages: account, addresses, wishlist, order success, order details, payment states, receipts/download labels, reviews, and notifications.
- Translate shared controls and dynamic interface states, including quantities, filters, sorting, delivery/payment choices, errors, loading text, and confirmation messages.
- Preserve product names, customer-entered content, addresses, order IDs, transaction IDs, brand names, prices, and other factual data unchanged.
- Keep the selected language remembered and update the document language for accessibility.

## Verification
- Check English, Telugu, and Hindi across desktop and mobile customer flows.
- Confirm no customer interface text remains in English after choosing Telugu or Hindi, except proper nouns and user/business data.
- Confirm the admin area remains unchanged and the app builds cleanly.
